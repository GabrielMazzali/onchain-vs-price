# MVRV_SENTIMENT_RESULTS.md - do valuation + fear flag good moments to buy?

Conditional forward returns by **MVRV state x sentiment state**, across horizons.
Tests the folklore: *'buy low MVRV'* and *'be greedy when others are fearful'*.
MVRV = look-ahead-safe expanding percentile (cheap <0.33, expensive >0.67);
sentiment = Fear&Greed (fear <45, greed >55). Generated by `python utils/mvrv_sentiment.py`.

## Bottom line - it depends entirely on the HORIZON

- **Short horizon (<=365d): momentum wins, the folklore LOSES.** 'Cheap + fear' underperforms; 'expensive + greed' outperforms. Buying into strength beat buying into fear.
- **Cycle horizon (540d ~1.5y): it FLIPS for BTC.** 'Cheap + fear' outperforms the baseline and 'expensive + greed' underperforms - the valuation/mean-reversion story, at the timescale these indicators are actually used on. **This retro-corrects the short-horizon 'cycle-timing refuted' finding** in `CYCLE_TIMING_RESULTS.md` / `REGIME_EVENT_RESULTS.md`: those were <=180d and thus horizon-blind.
- **BUT n is tiny at long horizons** (see `n_indep`): at 540d a 'state' spans ~1 independent episode (the 2022 bottom). This is a **case study of one cycle bottom, not a powered test.** And **ADA does not flip** even at 540d - the effect isn't universal. Properly testing a cycle rule needs multiple bottoms (=> history back to ~2011; note Fear&Greed only exists from 2018).

## Honest conclusion

Valuation + sentiment did **not** give a usable short-term buy signal (momentum dominated). At cycle horizons BTC shows the textbook reversal - buying cheap+fear and holding ~1.5 years beat the market - but this rests on **one** bear bottom (2022), does not replicate on ADA, and cannot be powered without more cycles of history. Best read as: *the valuation/sentiment 'buy the fear' rule is a long-cycle idea our ~1.5-cycle sample can illustrate for BTC but not statistically confirm.*
