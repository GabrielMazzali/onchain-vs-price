# SIGNAL_DETECTION_RESULTS.md - is there ANY on-chain -> price signal?

`VALIDATION_PLAN.md` step 3, model-free. Complements the ablation: instead of
asking whether a *model* captures on-chain signal, it asks whether the **data**
contains predictive structure at all. Three independent tools; q = Benjamini-
Hochberg FDR-adjusted p-value within each (asset, method).

Generated by `python utils/signal_detection.py`. Seeds pinned (`random_state=42`).

- **Lead-lag:** Pearson/Spearman of feature(t) vs forward return over 3d/7d/14d/30d. Significance uses **Newey-West** HAC SE (lag = horizon) because overlapping forward returns are autocorrelated; the naive iid p-value massively overstates it and is shown only for contrast.
- **Granger:** hand-rolled F-test, feature (first-differenced) -> next-day log return, lags (1, 3, 7, 14).
- **Mutual information:** feature(t) vs sign(fwd return 7d), empirical p from label shuffles.

## Bottom line

- **BTC** - survive FDR q<0.05: lead-lag(Newey-West) **28/172** (naive iid would wrongly say 67), granger **2/168**, MI **4/43**.
- **ADA** - survive FDR q<0.05: lead-lag(Newey-West) **23/144** (naive iid would wrongly say 77), granger **0/140**, MI **10/36**.

## Interpretation

- **Overlap correction matters most here.** The naive iid p-value flags dozens of correlations; Newey-West (which accounts for the autocorrelation of overlapping forward returns) removes ~60% of them. Never trust the iid p-value on overlapping returns.
- **What survives lead-lag is weak and price-shaped.** All surviving |Spearman r| <= 0.34 (economically tiny), and the survivors concentrate in `NVT_Tx_Basis`, `AdrActCnt`, `IssTotUSD`, `SplyCur` - valuation ratios that embed price (NVT, issuance-in-USD) and supply/activity trend proxies. These are essentially price/valuation effects, not independent on-chain information - which is exactly why the ablation found no lift from adding them on top of price.
- **Granger is the cleanest test** (it conditions on the return's own past and uses non-overlapping next-day returns). Its only survivors across both assets are `Price_vs_MA7` - **price** momentum features, no on-chain feature.
- **Mutual information flags 14 feature(s), but it is confounded by trend/regime.** The strongest are `SplyCur` (MI 0.37, trend 1.00), `ActiveStakeADA` (MI 0.30, trend 0.38), `StakingRatio` (MI 0.24, trend 0.56) - the highest-MI features are near-monotonic clocks (|Spearman vs time| > 0.7 for 4 of them), so their dependence with a regime-clustered return sign reflects *when* in the sample we are, not prediction. MI does not condition on the calendar or on price; Granger and the ablation, which do, find no on-chain edge. The remaining non-trending survivors are price-embedding valuation ratios (MVRV, NVT) and price volatility.

**Conclusion:** three independent model-free tests converge with the ablation - there is **no detectable incremental on-chain directional signal** for BTC/ADA at the daily frequency over 2020-2026. The only structure that survives proper correction is price-driven (valuation mean-reversion, price momentum). This is a strong, defensible negative result. (Any lingering interest in the ADA activity/supply features should go through the Blockfrost epoch->daily leak audit first, `VALIDATION_PLAN.md` Phase 3.3.)
